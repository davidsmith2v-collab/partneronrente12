Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Can both men and women register?

Yes. The concept allows eligible adults of any gender to create a companion profile and set their own rates, subject to platform verification and rules.

What does the ₹99 introduction include?

It is an example price for one short introductory phone conversation. The exact duration, taxes and payment terms must be shown before confirmation.

Can I meet a companion in person?

In-person social outings may be requested only where both adults freely agree. They must be non-sexual, take place under clear terms, and follow local laws and safety guidance.

Are the profiles on this preview real?

No. All profiles on this preview are sample content with illustrative photos, names, ratings and rates. A live platform must display only genuine, consented and verified profiles.

Can a companion choose their own price?

The intended model allows companions to propose rates within platform rules. The platform should show the total cost, duration, cancellation terms and payout terms before a booking is accepted.
